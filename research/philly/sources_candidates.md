# Philadelphia — candidate sources for approval (2026-09-21)

Nothing below has been pulled or read. Per the handoff, every non-Paulie source needs your yes
before its transcript is downloaded. Approve by tier, by video ID, or strike names off.

**How the list was built.** Two passes: (1) 23 YouTube searches (`tools/search_youtube.py`,
273 distinct videos, 143 flagged Philly by title/snippet, saved in `research/philly/search.json`);
(2) full video lists of the 14 channels that showed up most (`research/philly/channels/`), which
found 28 more Philly videos the search missed. For each, I fetched the description and whether an
auto-caption track exists (`candidates_meta.json`). No captions means music-only POV footage with
nothing to read, so those are listed but not recommended.

**What counts, same as NYC/LA:** shot-there and regular haunts by the person on camera. Visitors on
a one-day trip still count as shot-there, but they will never give a haunt, so they are a lower tier.
