# Philadelphia — proposed spots from Paulie B's two episodes (for review, nothing written yet)

Two Walkie Talkie episodes read in full: **Olu Okiemute** (eloT_IvSaOI, 37 min) and **Nazir Wayman**
(kQKkKCdwZA8, 62 min). Timestamped transcripts are in `research/philly/transcripts/`, reader JSON in
`research/philly/reader-results/`. Same rules as NYC and LA: shot-here and haunt count; biography, plans,
comparisons and city-level talk don't; one mention per guest per spot; high/medium only; pins need a
demonstrable frame at an exact place.

**How these were read (new this round).** Each episode was read twice by independent reader agents (one
tuned for recall, one strict), a third agent merged the two and re-read the transcript at every
disagreement, and a fourth agent tried to *refute* every mention against the transcript. Where the
refuter won, I say so and give my lean. The refuter was told to default to "drop" when unsure, so it is
stricter than the NYC readers were; treat its "drop" as a flag for you, not a verdict.

**The honest headline:** two episodes, both entirely in Center City. That gives **7 spots and 13
mentions** at my lean (up to 10 spots and 16 mentions if you keep everything the readers found), with
**3 high-confidence pins**. Both guests are locals with strong daily haunts, so the quality is good; the
coverage is one square mile. The candidate list in `research/philly/sources_candidates.md` is how the map
gets past Center City.

Coordinates are snapped to OpenStreetMap intersections (saved in `research/philly/osm_centercity.json`);
rounded to 5 decimals. Shapes are already built as a preview in `research/philly/boundaries_preview.js`
(nothing in `cities/` touched).

## C. Excluded, and why (the interesting ones)

- **La Colombe** (Nazir, 14:56, 38:44) — his daily coffee stop and where his friends are; a relationship,
  not a shooting spot (the Nico-shop rule). Captions garble it as "loom", "lock colom", "lack cologne".
- **Love Park / JFK Plaza** (Nazir, 33:47) — spoken only as skateboarding history ("the most famous one",
  "what they did over at Love Park"). He shoots the MSB, not Love Park. Notable because it is the famous
  one; nothing in the transcript puts a camera there.
- **City Hall Plaza** (Nazir, 33:53) — the demolished skate plaza, now Dilworth Park; history only.
- **Market East and Chinatown** (Olu, 1:22) — "if our feet are up for it"; never reached in the cut.
- **Atmos store, 1509 Walnut** (Nazir, 15:59) — former commercial client, closed; walked past.
- **Mummers Parade route and Two Street** (Nazir, 3:08–4:21) — annual event described, not shot today.
- **Kensington** (Nazir, 28:36) — Jeffrey Stockbridge's book. **North Philly, the stables, rec centers,
  Adrien Renee's neighborhood** (Nazir, 18:28–21:59) — other photographers' work. **Eugene Richards' North
  Philly backflip, Laurence Salzmann's City/2, Winogrand's Fifth Avenue and Mummers** — comparisons.
- **University of the Arts** (Nazir, 11:16), **operating BSL trains** (Olu, 3:50), **born and raised**
  (both), **"my neighborhood and the neighborhoods I go to with my family"** (Nazir, 55:12, unnamed) — biography.
- **FWS Gallery, 21st & Walnut** and the **Halide Project** (Olu, 25:45–27:40) — where he shows work.
- **The "standard street-photo-boy Philadelphia loop"** as a route-level spot (Olu, 1:08) — too lumped;
  its legs are counted individually (Chestnut, Rittenhouse, City Hall, the station).
