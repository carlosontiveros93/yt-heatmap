# Los Angeles — proposed spots (for review, nothing written yet)

Four LA Walkie Talkie episodes read in full (reader JSON in `research/la/reader-results/`,
timestamped transcripts in `research/la/transcripts/`). Same rules as NYC: shot-here and haunt
count; biography, past projects, comparisons and city-level talk don't; one mention per guest
per spot; high/medium confidence only; pins need a demonstrable frame at an exact place.

**The honest headline:** these four episodes are talk-heavy and geographically thin. Together
they give **10 spots and 16 mentions**, versus 78/173 for NYC. Only two spots (Hollywood Blvd,
Koreatown) have more than one photographer. The LA map will launch small; Paulie's other LA
content (the "where should visitors shoot in LA" style videos, if any) or other channels would be
the way to thicken it, same as the Philly plan.

Coordinates below are my estimates from street knowledge, rounded to 4 decimals (~10 m); every
one needs a look on the map before it is written, and every pin needs a look at the video.
