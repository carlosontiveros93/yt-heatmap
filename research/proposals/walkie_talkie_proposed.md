# Walkie Talkie — proposed additions (for review, nothing written yet)

16 episodes read in full. Rules applied on top of the readers' output: only **high** or
**medium** confidence claims; a place must be more specific than a borough; "shot here today"
and "regular haunt" both count; biography and one-off anecdotes don't. Where a guest both shot
somewhere today *and* called it a regular spot, that's **one** mention (the stronger quote).

Every mention would carry `type: "walkie-talkie"` plus a `basis` of `shot-here` or `haunt`,
a timestamp, and a quote, so the popup can say *who, what they said, and jump to the moment*.
